The Challenge

FROM FRAGMENTATION TO FLOW

Embracing Hawker Culture to shape brand

The challenge for the brand was to embody hawker culture through an app that provides a seamless user experience. The goal was to create a cohesive and personal ecosystem that users can easily engage with everyday.

A shared visual language bridging brand and product experiences for users.

In addition to shaping the visual identity, I directed UI/UX for product features, capturing the spirit of hawker culture and prioritizing human intentions over mere service categories. By engaging stakeholders and conducting thorough research, I highlighted brand values that genuinely connect with our audience, while also establishing accessibility standards to promote inclusivity.
