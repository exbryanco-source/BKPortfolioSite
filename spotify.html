A 10-Year Anniversary Campaign

A love-letter to Mandopop in the shape of a campaign.

The Challenge

FROM FRAGMENTATION TO FLOW

Celebrating Mandopop Music

To celebrate 10 years of Spotify in Taiwan, we started the #MyMandopopMemory challenge that transported listeners back to the good ol’ days while helping them create new memories to look back to. The best part? Everyone’s favourite Mandopop artists were in on the reminiscing too!

Mandopop has been with listeners in every milestone of their lives

Through content pieces that trigger oh-so-good nostalgic feels and ones that introduce emerging music, we celebrate the diverse stories of artists and listeners 10 years into the past and future.
