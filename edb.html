Branding business in Singapore

A modernisation of a local heritage brand, representing business on a global stage.

The Challenge

FROM FRAGMENTATION TO FLOW

The Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB) is the government agency responsible for enhancing Singapore’s position as a global centre for business, innovation and talent.

Over a decade had passed since its last brand update, and with Brand SG’s launch, it was an opportune time for EDB to introduce a new look that reinforced what it stood for since 1961.

Bridging brand & motion

I had the opportunity to design compelling brand animations for the rebranding exercise, creating an animation kit for seamless implementation across various media, including brand videos, interviews, and more.
