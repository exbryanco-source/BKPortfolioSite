Campus Hotel Branding: The Ridge at NUS

A destination brand for guests attending academic programs and events at NUS.

The Opportunity

2026

NUS and Tangible embarked on new executive accommodation offer, located in the heart of the NUS Kent Ridge Campus.

The opportunity was to create a destination brand for guests attending academic programs and events, comparable to a mid-tier business hotel.

Brand Identity & Communications

Tangible developed the name, visual and verbal identity, communication templates, signage, website, activation plan, image library and launch video for The Ridge.
