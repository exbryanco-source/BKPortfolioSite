Brand refresh for a global transport & logistics company.

The Challenge

FROM FRAGMENTATION TO FLOW

A strategy to build a future focused business

The challenge for the brand was to represent a brand with over 12,000 employees and uniting them under one share purpose.

A logo change for any business with a proud heritage involves careful stakeholder engagement

An evolution of the logo proved to be the most effective way forward for PIL, with the waves in the symbol rationalised to reflect their three values and bold personality. The Visual & Verbal language was revitalised, together with a full suite of communication material to signal their ‘customer-centric’ approach and confident personality.
